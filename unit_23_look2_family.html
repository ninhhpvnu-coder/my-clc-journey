A Saturday Trip to the Town Centre

Today is Saturday, and Ben is going to the town centre with his family. His dad wears a blue shirt and dark trousers. His mum has got a pretty straw hat. Ben’s big brother wears a warm red jacket because the morning wind is cool.

First, they visit a fruit shop next to the park. Mum buys six sweet yellow bananas. Then Dad says, "Let’s eat some cold ice cream!" Ben and his little brother feel very happy. There are many children playing happily in the green park.

In the afternoon, they return home. Ben goes to his bedroom to read his favourite comic book. On the wall, there’s a big poster of a sea turtle.
