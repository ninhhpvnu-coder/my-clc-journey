Getting Around the Coastal City

Tony lives in a coastal city. Every morning, he gets ready early to get to school. His school is not far, so he rides his bicycle past the central library and the city swimming pool. Other students travel by yellow buses or take the morning train.

At break time, Tony eats a healthy snack. Today, he has a bunch of grapes from a wooden box. After class, Tony and his sister come home and help their mother wash the lunch dishes. On weekends, their father takes them to the beach near the old shipyard. They watch big ships that come from Europe across the ocean.
