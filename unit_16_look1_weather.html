🌾 The Friendly Scarecrow and the Autumn Harvest

Autumn is harvest time at Green Meadow Farm, and the weather is crisp and cool.

In the middle of the golden rice field stands Mr. Straw, a very happy scarecrow. Farmer John dressed Mr. Straw in an old dark blue shirt, grey trousers, and a wide straw hat. On his hands, he wears red woolen gloves, and on his feet are black rubber boots. Two friendly sparrows sit right on his straw shoulder because they know Mr. Straw has a kind heart.

Near the farmhouse, clean clothes and white school uniforms dry quickly on the long washing line. At noon, the farmer's family gathers around the wooden table to eat a delicious harvest lunch with steamed rice, mashed potatoes, fresh bananas, and warm lemon tea. All the workers feel so lucky and thankful for a bountiful harvest season!
