How We Stay Fit and Active

Keeping fit is important for every young player. Many children like to play on their tablets after school, but sitting inside for too much time is not healthy. I think students should go outside and play sport together.

My friend Linh and I love badminton and table tennis. We practice every Tuesday. Sometimes, we go for a walk in the green park with our families. Linh wears her favourite pink sports shoes. Her older brother is in a circus troupe. He can ride a tall unicycle very well! Maybe next month, he will teach us how to balance on it.

Do you want to stay healthy and active? Just join a school team, score a goal, and have great fun!
