Remarkable Journeys to School

Every morning, children around the world make an amazing journey to school. In Caracas, the capital of Venezuela, students ride a modern cable car high above the bustling streets. From up there, they look down at colourful houses next to green trees. However, in remote villages near the Himalayas in India, going to school is not easy. Children walk a long way on foot, passing through dense forests and along freezing rivers. During the monsoon season, the path becomes very dangerous, so older students hold hands and walk together all the way. In big cities like Paris in France, kids simply take the subway under the ground. Though their journeys are different, every child is eager to learn.
