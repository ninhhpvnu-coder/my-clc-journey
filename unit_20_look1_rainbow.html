🌈 The Magic Colour Wheel and the Rainbow Experiment

Today in Art and Science class, teacher Elena shows us a magical experiment about the science of rainbow colours.

First, Miss Elena asks, 'Can everybody name the three primary colours?' We raise our hands and answer together, 'Red, blue, and yellow!' She nods happily and gives each pair of students a ceramic palette and a spinning colour wheel.

'Now, let's explore what happens when we mix them!' she says. By mixing blue and yellow, we create fresh green grass. By mixing red and yellow, we produce a bright orange sunset. When we add white paint, the colours become lighter; when we add a tiny drop of black, they turn darker. We imagine an enchanted landscape and paint a glorious rainbow arching over a quaint French pastry shop. Learning how colours interact is truly pure magic!
