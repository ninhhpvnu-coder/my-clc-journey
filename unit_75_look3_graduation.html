Maya's Award-Winning Island Adventure

Last December, an adventurous photographer named Maya packed her suitcase to spend two weeks on a volcanic island in the Pacific Ocean. Her dream was to capture dramatic pictures of an active volcano and win a national photography award. Each morning at camp, she would pour hot tea from an iron pot before hiking up the rocky slopes. During her trek, she had to decide which path was safe to avoid falling rocks. While Maya was taking a photo of a rare tropical bird, a playful monkey jumped into the frame to photobomb the shot! Maya submitted that funny photo as her official competition entry. Surprisingly, the humorous picture won the top award, proving that unexpected moments often bring the greatest joy.
