An Adventure on the Mountain Road

Last week, Lan and her brother Nam took an exciting bus trip to the high mountains of Sa Pa. At the beginning of their journey, their mother reminded them, "The mountain road has many steep curves, so you must hold on carefully."

The experienced driver drove the vehicle slowly through a long, dark tunnel. Outside the window, they saw a cyclist riding near a clear mountain stream. Smooth stones by the water shone like sparkling jewels under the bright morning sun.

Later that evening, after eating a warm dinner at a local restaurant, Lan opened her diary. She wrote a heartfelt thank-you note to their careful driver for keeping everyone safe on the road.
