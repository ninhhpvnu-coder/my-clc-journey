Unique Ways to Get Around

There are many exciting types of transport to help people get around the world. In Bangkok, men and women often ride in a colourful tuk-tuk through busy streets. In Venice, the roads are wet canals, so children travel by traditional gondola.

High in the snowy mountains, a cable car carries visitors up into the clouds. Passengers must fasten their seat belts safely. From high above, the houses below look tiny, and a flock of white sheep looks like little dots of wool. Even though their feet feel cold, travelers wear thick scarves and write their adventures in a notebook.
