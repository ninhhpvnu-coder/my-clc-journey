⚽ Tom's Family and Their Jobs

Many people have interesting jobs. Tom’s father is a taxi driver. He drives a taxi in the city and works very hard. Tom’s mother teaches young children at a small school.

Tom’s uncle is a German football player. He scores goals in a big stadium, and millions of people all over the world watch him play. He does not use a car. He takes a bus to training.

Tom is eight years old. He wants to become a football player too, so he practises every day. His mother says, "You must work hard, Tom. Then you can grow up and have a great job!"
