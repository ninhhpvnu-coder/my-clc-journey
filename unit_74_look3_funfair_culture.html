Liam's Summer Camp Adventure

Last July, Liam joined an exciting summer camp in the green mountains of Scotland. The campers stayed in a comfortable wooden cabin near a sparkling waterfall. Every morning, Liam practiced new outdoor skills like kayaking down the clear river and camping under the stars. On Wednesday, the friendly camp chef prepared a wonderful evening feast with grilled vegetables and fresh bread for everyone. Later that week, their guide bought a group ticket for a memorable trip to a nearby wildlife park, where children watched majestic red deer roam freely. Liam loved learning natural history and wrote every exciting event on his personal calendar. It was a holiday he would never forget.
