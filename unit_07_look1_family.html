🎂 Lucas's Big Family Birthday Party

My name is Lucas and I am a Brazilian boy living in Brasilia. Today is my tenth birthday, and my house is full of laughter.

I've got a very big family. My grandma and grandpa travel from the countryside to visit us. In the middle of the living room, dad and mum prepare a huge chocolate birthday cake with ten bright orange candles.

My uncle, aunt, and three cousins arrive with colourful gift boxes. My cousin Maria gives me a funny toy monkey that climbs a plastic tree. We count together from one to ten, and I blow out all the candles in one breath. Everyone claps their hands and shouts, 'Happy Birthday, Lucas!'
