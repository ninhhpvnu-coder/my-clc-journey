A Special Family Dinner in the City

Every Saturday evening, Linh’s family likes to eat out together as a special weekend treat. Yesterday, they went to a popular family restaurant near the city central park. The restaurant was quite busy, but the friendly waiters quickly found them a cosy table next to a sparkling water fountain. Linh ordered a hot bowl of chicken noodles with fresh vegetables, while her brother chose a grilled beef burger and a colourful plate of green salad. For dessert, they shared sweet strawberry pancakes and drank cold fruit milkshake through paper straws instead of plastic ones. Their mother explained that paper straws protect nature and our marine environment. It was a wonderful meal, and they packed the leftover pizza into the fridge when they returned home.
