Mr Dan and His Ocean Adventures

Mr Dan is a famous scuba diver. Today, he is working off the coast of Phu Quoc Island. The water is warm and clear. Before he jumps into the sea, he puts on his black mask and two blue flippers.

Under the water, Mr Dan can swim with many colourful fish. He uses his strong arms and legs to move quickly. Look! There are ten small yellow fish near the coral reef. How many people are there on the boat? There are four friends waiting for him.

After an hour, Mr Dan walks out of the sea and takes a warm shower. He always tells the children, "Remember to keep our sea clean!"
