A Fun International Food Party

Today, our school has a big festival to celebrate International Food Day. We have a party in the sunny schoolyard and everyone wants to have fun.

First, our class wants to draw lovely posters. Lan uses a red crayon to color a warm pancake with sweet chocolate sauce. Next, the teachers cook delicious dishes from many countries. Ben eats some fried potato chips with onion sauce. His brother tries a Mexican tortilla with mild chili sauce. Everyone says the food is very tasty!

Now, cheerful children dance to lively songs and listen to music. Mum and Dad drink cold lemonade and take photos of us. My friends are happy because they’re holding big yellow balloons.
