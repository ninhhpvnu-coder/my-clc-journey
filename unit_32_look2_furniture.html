Our Dream House Plan

Can you imagine our dream house? My brother Tony and I drew a special plan for it yesterday. In our plan, we live with our parents in a big eco-house.

Inside the house, we’ve got a soft green rug on the wooden floor and a big mirror in the hall. We haven’t got a normal lift, but we have got colorful stairs! In our bedroom, there is a super tall climbing wall between two bookcases. Tony can climb up to his bed.

Outside, in front of the house, there is a green yard with a wooden swing. Behind the house, our cat loves to chase and catch a little mouse before lunch.
