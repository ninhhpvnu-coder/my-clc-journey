A Fun Basketball Match

Nam lives in a modern flat in a tall building. From his living room sofa, he has a beautiful view of the sports playground below. Every schoolchild in his neighborhood loves going down to play there after class.

Today, Nam meets his friend Phong at the court. Basketball is very popular at their school. They play on the same team. Nam learns to bounce the orange ball and throw it into the basket. His grandfather comes to watch and cheers loudly. Nam says, "It is not easy to score, but we try our best!"

Their coach says, "Don’t kick the ball; this is basketball, not football!" Nam passes the ball to Phong, and Phong scores two points. They finish the fantastic game with big smiles.
