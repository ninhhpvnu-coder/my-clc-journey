The Multicultural School Sleepover and Fashion Show

Last weekend, Oakridge Middle School hosted a memorable charity sleepover party to celebrate cultural diversity and raise funds for community projects. Every student received a formal invitation with guidelines to pack essential personal hygiene items, including a toothbrush, a clean towel, and mild organic soap. When dusk settled, the gymnasium was transformed into a bustling hall where classmates laid out sleeping bags and thick blankets to keep themselves warm against the chilly night breeze. The crowning highlight of the gathering was a vibrant fashion show. Participants proudly showcased traditional garments inherited from their ancestors or designed using recycled textiles. Layla wore an exquisite green gandoura that once belonged to her great-grandma in North Africa. Nearby, Kevin playfully modelled a pair of odd socks that did not match his shoes, sparking hearty laughter across the room. During the wardrobe check, an instructor asked, 'Whose smartphone is this left on the bench?', to which Sarah politely replied, 'It isn't mine; I believe it is theirs.' The headteacher commended the friendly spirit, reminding everyone that celebrating differences stops any cruel bully, ensures no vulnerable child feels weak or unhappy, and helps protect our shared school community.
