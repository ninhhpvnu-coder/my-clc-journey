Guardians of the Coast: Young Eco-Heroes in Action

Along the rugged coastline of Blue Bay, fifteen-year-old Liam has become a recognized eco-hero among local residents. Witnessing tons of discarded plastic bottles, old rubber tyre pieces, and single-use straw items wash up on the sandy shore, Liam decided that teenagers could make a lasting difference. Last summer, he mobilized his school friends to organize a massive coastal clean-up campaign. To encourage eco-friendly transport, the volunteers rode along the newly built cycle path every morning to reach the bay. Guided by Dr. Elena Ramos, a dedicated marine biologist, the youth team learned how plastic debris threatens marine wildlife and vulnerable coastal species. Instead of simply throwing the collected rubbish away, Liam collaborated with art teachers to create an impressive outdoor sculpture of a sea turtle, placing an informative sign beside it to raise public awareness. Dr. Ramos explained that pelagic animals such as swordfish, giant mola mola, and bottom-dwelling sea cucumber populations depend heavily on uncontaminated habitats. The group works tirelessly to make sure that visitors respect ocean life and actively look after coastal ecosystems for future generations.
