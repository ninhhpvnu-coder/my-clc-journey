Fascinating Habitats & Nature's Balance

Nature is full of incredible creatures living in different habitats. In the tropical rainforest, bright macaws fly among tall branches while monkeys climb a tree to find sweet fruit. These wild animals should live happily in the wild instead of being kept as a pet.

Far away in the dry desert, little meerkats leave their underground burrow each morning. They stand together under the sunshine to warm up their bodies and relax. Meanwhile, in the icy ocean of the Antarctic, penguins and great whales depend on tiny krill for vital energy. Every amazing species plays an important role in our planet’s food chain.
