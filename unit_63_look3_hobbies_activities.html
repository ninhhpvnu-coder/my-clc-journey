Creative Hobbies for Happy Weekends

Hobbies make our lives colourful and exciting after busy school days. During playtime at school, pupils often play hide-and-seek or read a funny comic book together. At home, teenagers have many creative ways to relax. Linh is deeply interested in arts and crafts, so she loves to make things from recycled materials. Instead of throwing away empty plastic bottles, she cleans them and recycles them into flower pots or pencil holders. Her brother Nam enjoys outdoor hobbies; on windy afternoons, he goes to the open park to fly a large dragon kite. In the quiet evening, after finishing every household chore, the whole family gathers in the living room to do puzzles. Having a healthy hobby brings joy and keeps young minds active.
