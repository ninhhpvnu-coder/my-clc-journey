A Cosy Sunday at Home

Today is Sunday, and my family is at home. In the spacious living room, my grandpa and grandma are relaxing on the sofa. My uncle and aunt are cooking lunch in the kitchen. My older brother is in the bathroom washing his hands.

My cousin Linh is also here. She is my favourite classmate at school. We are sitting at the desk under a bright lamp. It’s time for our learning to begin. Linh asks, "Have you got a pen or a pencil?" I say, "No, I haven’t. But we can get one from my brother." Then we open our English book to have a look at the pictures. We both like studying together.
