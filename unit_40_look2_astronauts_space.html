Life on the Space Station

What is life like for an astronaut in outer space? On the International Space Station, astronauts live four hundred kilometres above our blue Earth. Because there is no gravity, they float through the air! They look out of the windows and see the dark space and millions of stars.

Daily life in space includes hard work and fun. Every morning and night, astronauts brush each tooth and wash with wet towels. They must do exercise for two hours every day. Sometimes, they even make special space food! Astronauts cannot twirl soft pizza dough like real chefs, but they can eat flat bread with cheese, beans, and sweet fruits like a grape or a pear. They are really happy when families visit their control room from Earth.
