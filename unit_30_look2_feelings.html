A Great Day at the Museum

Nam and his sister Lan are at the National Museum today. They feel happy because they can see a big dinosaur. There is also a room about new technology and innovation.

After one hour, Nam is tired. Lan is hungry and thirsty. Mum asks, "What’s the matter?" Lan says, "We have nothing to eat." Mum finds a snack in her bag. There are two biscuits and one apple. "Here you are," she says.

Now the children feel great. Lan gives the biscuits to Nam. He says, "Dear Lan, I only want one!" Then they say bye to the dinosaur.
