Fascinating Festivals Around the World

People around the world enjoy exciting holidays throughout the year. In April, Thai people celebrate their traditional New Year. Everyone goes onto the busy street for a lively water fight. Children fill each big bucket with cool water to splash on their friends.

In autumn, American families celebrate Thanksgiving together. They feel grateful for their loved ones and share roasted turkey with sweet pumpkin pie. In Britain, people remember Bonfire Night in November with fires and fireworks.

During a summer carnival, performers wear bright masks and hold hands in a long parade. Visitors find a good seat to watch them or have a family picnic in the green park. These celebrations end with beautiful music and joy.
