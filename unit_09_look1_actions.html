🎉 Staying Fit and Healthy at the School Sports Festival

Every October, our primary school holds a colourful Sports and Fitness Festival in the large stadium.

All students wear white sports shirts and comfortable running shoes. First, our PE teacher, Mr. Long, leads the warm-up session. He says loudly, 'Nod your heads, wave your arms, and jump high in the air!' Everyone jumps together, stamps their feet to the music, and laughs heartily.

Next, there is a fun race called 'Keep Fit Together'. My classmate Mai has got long black hair tied in a ponytail. She runs like a gazelle and points to the finish line with a confident smile. Our team wins first prize, and the headmistress presents us with shiny gold medals. Doing regular exercises keeps our bodies strong and energetic!
