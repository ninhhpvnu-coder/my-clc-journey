From Little Chicks to Big Flamingos

Animals change a lot during their life. In the warm Caribbean, a mother flamingo sits on her nest near a quiet pond. Soon, a little grey chick comes out of the egg. At first, it is not pink, and its legs are very short. The chick is never naughty. It stays clean and listens to its parents.

As time passes, the little chick eats tiny pond animals. Then an amazing change happens! Its grey feathers become bright pink, and it grows huge wings. Now, the chick is a famous, beautiful bird. Its baby days are completely gone, and it is ready to live like a clever grown-up.
