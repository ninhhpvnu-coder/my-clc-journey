🗽 An Unforgettable Summer Holiday from City to Farm

Last July, our family had a two-week holiday in the United States.

During the first week, we explored New York City. We took a ferry ride to see the colossal Statue of Liberty up close. Dad bought a colourful city map, and we visited the Metropolitan Museum of Art. The buildings were so tall that they touched the clouds!

In the second week, we visited uncle Robert's countryside farm. It was quiet and peaceful. Uncle Robert has got twenty sheep, five cows, two donkeys, and lots of yellow chickens. Every morning, I helped uncle feed the sheep and listened to birds singing from the apple trees. Uncle Robert's border collie dog is very clever; it can run fast and guide all twenty sheep safely into the pen. It was a truly special holiday!
