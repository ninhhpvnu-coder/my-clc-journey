📖 A Wonderful Day at the Safari Zoo

Today, class 5A visits the city safari zoo. All the children are very excited.

First, their tour guide shows them the giant elephants. The elephants are very big and gentle. They use their long trunks to spray water and eat sweet green grass. Next, the children walk to the blue lake where hundreds of colorful fish are swimming. Near the lake, there is a green hill with playful goats and strong horses running freely.

The guide says, 'Remember to be kind and look after animals. Do not throw food into their enclosures, please!' The students listen carefully, take photos, and write notes in their science journals.
