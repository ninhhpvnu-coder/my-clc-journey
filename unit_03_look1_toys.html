📖 A Fun Visit to the Big Toy Shop

On Saturday morning, Mai and her little brother Huy go to the big toy shop with their father. The shop is really big and colorful.

Huy loves vehicles. He runs directly to the shelf with toy trains and airplanes. He sees a long silver train with eight small wheels. 'Is it fast, Dad?' Huy asks excitedly. 'Yes, it is very fast!' his father replies with a smile.

Mai walks to the other side of the shop. She sees lovely teddy bears and colorful kites. Mai chooses a soft brown teddy bear with a red ribbon around its neck. Both children are very happy because their father lets them share their new toys at home!
