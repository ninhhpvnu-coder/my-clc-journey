Helping Wildlife and Family at Home

During the freezing winter, life is not easy for wild creatures in our northern land. Many birds migrate south to find warm weather, but small garden birds remain. Every weekend morning before breakfast, Minh helps out his family and nature. He always checks a colourful weather map to make sure conditions are safe outside. Then, he goes into the snowy garden to look after the birds and feed them dry seeds. Minh does this chore twice a day. In the afternoon, he usually cleans the house, puts away his books and takes out the rubbish for recycling. Sometimes, Minh waters the green houseplants on the windowsill. By doing these chores, he learns to care for living things and keeps his home tidy.
