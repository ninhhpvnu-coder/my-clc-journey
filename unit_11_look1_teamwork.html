🎻 A Wonderful Music and Teamwork Lesson in the Hall

Every Wednesday morning, the fifth-grade students gather in the school music hall.

Today, our music teacher, Miss Lan, introduces a special violin performance. 'Are you ready to learn?' she asks with a gentle smile. All thirty students nod happily. Miss Lan shows us how to hold the bow and play simple musical notes that sound like singing birds.

After that, we work in groups of four to complete a music exercise on page eighteen of our workbook. My friend Nam forgets his pencil and says politely, 'May I borrow your pencil, please?' I pass him my green pencil and say, 'Here you are!' We take turns writing the correct answers. When we work together, learning English and Music is both easy and fun!
