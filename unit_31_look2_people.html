A Visit to the Cob House

Mr and Mrs Brown live in a lovely cob house in the countryside. The house has got a big wooden door and a bright dining room. In the living room, there is a comfortable armchair next to a tall bookcase.

Today, they have got two special visitors: their daughter and her new baby girl. The baby has got big brown eyes, short hair, and a small mouth. When the man and woman smile at her, she moves her tiny fingers and smiles back. In the afternoon, both parents go outside. The children dance and hop on the green grass, while their dog kicks a small red ball.
