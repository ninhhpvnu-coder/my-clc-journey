An Unforgettable Journey Across Italy

Last summer, my family took an exciting journey across Italy. We started in Rome, the famous capital city full of ancient buildings and stone bridges. In Rome, we enjoyed fresh pasta at a friendly family restaurant.

Then, we travelled to the quiet countryside where my grandparents live in a small village. A narrow path led us through a green forest to a breathtaking waterfall. The natural lake below was crystal clear. We sat near the cool river and watched thousands of wild birds fly above the high mountain. On our way home, Dad filled our bottles with cold water from a natural mountain spring. It was the best summer holiday ever.
