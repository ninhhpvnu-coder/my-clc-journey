An Unforgettable Road Trip

Last summer, Tom and his family went on an unforgettable road trip. On the first day of the week, they began their journey towards the mountains. Tom loved reading an exciting adventure story while his father drove. In the story, brave explorers sailed down the Amazon and tried to climb high peaks like Mount Fuji. During their road trip, Tom’s family decided to stay at a small hotel between two green hills. They ate fresh soup with sweet peas for dinner and watched a funny movie together. Before going to bed, Tom wrote the date in his journal and put a full stop after every line. He followed each rule his teacher taught him.
