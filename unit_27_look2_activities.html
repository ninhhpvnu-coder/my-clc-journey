After-School Activities and Family Fun

Today is Friday, and the students have lots of fun after school. Nam and Minh play football in the green park. Nam is very good at football, and his teacher is proud of him.

At five o’clock, the boys come home. Nam is being responsible today. He helps his mother make a delicious dinner in the kitchen. Then he gives his white cat a warm bath.

"Phew! That was hard work," says Nam. His mother smiles and gives him a cup of sweet tea. Now Nam can do his favourite activity: watch TV with his family on these soft mats. It is a wonderful evening.
