Lucy and the Hurt Magpie

Every day, Lucy gets up at six o’clock in the morning. She gets dressed and has breakfast with her family. Today, while walking to school, Lucy finds a small magpie on the grass. The poor bird's wing is hurt, so Lucy gently puts it into a warm shoe box.

In the afternoon, Lucy and her father take the bird to a vet. They wait quietly in the room. The vet says, "With food and care, this bird will feel much better soon." Lucy gives the magpie a nice meal of seeds and water.

In the evening, after Lucy does homework and has dinner, she checks on her new friend. The bird chirps happily again. Lucy loves animals and goes to bed with a happy smile.
