My Fun Week at School

My name is Nam, and I am very happy to go back to school. At school, every day is exciting. On Monday and Tuesday, I’ve got maths and science. I like science because we learn many things about animals and plants.

On Wednesday, we have reading and art. We draw colourful pictures of flowers, and that’s so cool! On Thursday, we play basketball in PE and sing songs in music. On Friday, we learn how to type on computers in English. My friend asks, "Do you like school?" and I say, "Yes, I do!"

On Saturday and Sunday, there’s no school. Please touch your nose if you love the weekend too!
