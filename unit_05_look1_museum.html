🏛️ Our Exciting Field Trip to the National Museum

Today is an unforgettable day for class 5B. At eight o'clock, our teacher and forty students go to the National History Museum by bus.

When we arrive, we line up outside the front gate. A friendly woman gives each student a museum map and says, 'Please be quiet and do not run inside.'

The first exhibition room is full of giant dinosaur models. There is a huge dinosaur that is ten metres tall! In the craft corner, our teacher helps us make colorful paper animals. At twelve o'clock, we say 'Goodbye!' to the museum guide and return home with joyful memories.
