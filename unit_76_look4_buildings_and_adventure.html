An Unforgettable Eco-Camp in Wolong Valley

Last July, Kevin left the noisy city where modern skyscrapers reached into the clouds to spend his holiday in a quiet mountain village. The village was famous for a wildlife reserve that protected the rare giant panda. Kevin joined a local summer camp organized near a winding river. Each camper pitched a small waterproof tent under the tall pine trees. Kevin's camp leader was Mr. Davis, an energetic guide who had a thick moustache, wore black glasses, and had short curly hair. Every morning, the campers packed a reusable bottle of water and went canoeing on the calm lake before hiking. Kevin was amazed to observe two giant pandas eating fresh bamboo shoots near a wooden bridge. In the evening, everyone gathered around the camp to eat outside. They enjoyed hot plates of homemade pasta while sharing folk stories. As darkness fell, hundreds of tiny bats flew out from rocky caves into the night sky. The peaceful environment helped Kevin forget his previous cough and stomach pain, giving him unforgettable memories of outdoor life.
