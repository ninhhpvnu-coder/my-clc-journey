An Excavation and a Mountain Adventure

Last summer, Lucas joined a fascinating school trip to an ancient village with a famous archaeologist. They explored the stone remains of old European houses and discovered where their ancestors lived centuries ago. Lucas saw an antique brick oven and learnt inspiring stories about traditional culture. The village was located in a green valley where the mountain air was crisp and pure.

At noon, the students sat under the trees nearby to eat outside and have a pleasant picnic. Later, Lucas experienced a real adventure when the group decided to go canoeing on the river. They followed their guide carefully so they would not lose their way in the forest. At night, lying near the camp, Lucas was thrilled to see a shooting star streak across the clear sky.
