Staying Healthy at School

An is an active student at an elementary school in Da Nang. Last month, he studied very hard to get a good grade in his final exams. However, he started to worry too much and felt ill. One morning, his mother asked, "What’s the matter, An?" He replied that his neck was sore and he had a bad stomach ache.

His doctor gave him some medicine and gave him helpful advice. In the doctor's opinion, children must keep both their body and mind calm and healthy. The doctor advised An to avoid sugary drinks that harm his teeth. An also decided to give yoga a go to relax every evening. Now, he feels energetic again and never forgets to visit the dentist regularly.
