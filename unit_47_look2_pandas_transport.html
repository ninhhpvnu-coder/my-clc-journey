A Visit to the Zoo and the Giant Pandas

Last Sunday, our family went to the city zoo by bus. The streets were very busy with many cars, motorbikes, and a big yellow lorry. When we arrived, Dad said, "Excuse me, where can we see the giant panda?"

A kind zookeeper told us to follow the path past the tall bamboo trees. She explained that each panda eats many kilograms of green bamboo every day. During the dry season and before the rainy monsoon, the zookeepers study animal habits to understand them better. My sister said, "I’d like a bottle of apple juice now." Then we took a photo of the pandas eating by themselves. It was an exciting trip.
