A Great Visit to the Natural History Museum

Last Saturday morning, Peter and his sister Freja traveled by car to a natural history museum in Denmark. Peter is tall with short straight hair, while Freja has a cute round face and long curly hair. Their grandfather, who has a grey beard, guided them through the exhibition.

They saw a giant skeleton of a leopard seal from Antarctica. An expert told them that an adult leopard seal can measure nearly 300 centimetres long. Their food includes fish, penguins, and smaller seals. Peter wondered how these creatures survive in freezing water. After the visit, they ate sweet pastries that tasted delicious, learning to accept and respect the amazing differences in nature.
