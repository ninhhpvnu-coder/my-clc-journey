Breakfast Around the World

Every country has delicious food for the morning. In Turkey, a typical breakfast has fresh bread, white cheese, eggs, cucumbers and red tomatoes. Turkish people also drink warm black tea. In Japan, a Japanese breakfast is very healthy. People often eat rice, soup, fish and green beans.

At our school, students eat different things before coming by bus. Nam always eats crunchy cereal with cold milk. His sister Mai sometimes eats a sweet pastry and drinks orange juice. After breakfast, the children meet in the playground until the morning bell rings. Mai says, "Do you want to play a game?" They laugh happily together.
