A Bug Safari at the Nature Reserve

Today, Minh and his sister Mai visit a large nature reserve in the countryside. A friendly keeper named Mr Hai shows them around. The children want to go on a mini-safari and look for small creatures.

First, they spot a shiny green beetle on a tree trunk. Nearby, a colourful dragonfly with four thin wings darts across the pond. Mr Hai tells them it is a blue dasher. In the tall grass, they also see a huge golden orb-weaver. Minh learns that a spider is an arachnid, unlike an insect that has six legs.

At the end of the path, they wave hello to a giant panda eating fresh bamboo. Minh says, "My dirty clothes show that science in nature is so exciting!"
