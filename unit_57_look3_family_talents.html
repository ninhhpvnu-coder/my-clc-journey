A Wildlife Expert Visits Our Class

Last Friday, our class welcomed Dr. Weber, a wildlife expert from Germany. He shared fascinating stories about his research in Namibia. Dr. Weber showed us photos of giraffes with unique dark spots and soft coats. He explained that a tall giraffe cannot hide easily, but its pattern helps it blend into the trees.

Dr. Weber has a young son and a daughter. They often travel with their parents by train or plane. His daughter is an artist who likes to paint animals, while his son wants to be a sports player. At the end of the lesson, our teacher invited him to try pho, a delicious traditional Vietnamese dish.
