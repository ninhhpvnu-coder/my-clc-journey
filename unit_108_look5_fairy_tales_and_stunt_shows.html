A Night of Fairy Tales and Circus Magic

Every winter, our town holds a pantomime based on a famous fairy tale. This year, the story is about a king and a queen who want their daughter to get married. The actors perform on a big stage, and a puppet plays the part of a talking cat. Before the show, the ringmaster of the small circus next door invites the audience to watch some incredible acrobatics. A young acrobat walks along a tightrope, jumps through a hoop and then flies from one trapeze to another. Last year, a girl hurt her knee because she skipped her warm-up exercises, so now everyone must prepare carefully. The director's intention is to give children the chance to enjoy art and forget their problems for an evening. Many families prefer this show to going to the cinema because life in a busy city is stressful, and live performances bring people together. When the play ends, the actors exit the stage, the crowd shouts "Hurrah!" and everybody goes home with a smile.
