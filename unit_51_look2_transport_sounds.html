The Story of the Bicycle

Before bicycles existed, people made each journey on foot or on a strong horse. Then in the nineteenth century, inventors created the first bicycle. It had two wooden wheels and was difficult to ride.

Later, inventors decided to make better bikes. They put soft rubber tyres filled with air onto each wheel. In the front, there were handlebars to steer. People also loved the new invention because it did not scare animals. By the 1950s, more than one hundred million people rode bicycles every day. Today, there are amazing electric bicycles, and people do not need horses anymore.
