A Peaceful Sunday at Home

Mai lives in a pretty house with a small green garden. Her father is an engineer and her mother is a doctor at the city hospital. On Sunday morning, her mother cooks a delicious meal, and the family eat rice, vegetables, and sweet pears. Afterwards, Dad sits in his soft armchair to read books from the wooden bookcase, while Mai and her brother sit on the sofa to watch TV.

In the afternoon, Mai sits at her computer to do her homework. Her favourite school subject is English, but she also likes PE. She uses colourful felt-tip pens to draw pictures of children from countries around the world. Mai feels happy when her weekend is full of joy.
