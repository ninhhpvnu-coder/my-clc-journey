A Daring Rescue on Misty Mountain

Last October, a group of young scouts travelled by coach to Misty Ridge for an outdoor physical training trip. At first, the weather was bright as the sun began to rise over the green hills, and everyone put on their sunglasses. The park ranger led them past a clear stream near a fish farm, where they were thrilled to spot a wild otter chasing a salmon. During lunch break, the students were allowed to sit on a fallen log and do their own thing. However, early in the afternoon, the weather turned dangerous. A sudden storm rolled in, bringing thick fog that covered the cliffs. Instead of staying calm, two scouts ignored advice and walked ahead. One boy fell over a hidden rock, cut his leg and broke his arm. Fortunately, their guide blew his whistle loudly to summon the mountain rescue team. With admirable patience and skill, the rescuers reached the injured boy. They opened their first aid kit, wrapped a clean bandage around his wrist, applied a plaster to his cut, and placed a protective helmet on his head before carrying him down to safety.
