🎨 An Art Lesson with Our Best Friends

At our school, Friday afternoon is our favorite time because we have an Art lesson with Mr. David.

Today, Mr. David shows us a famous Italian painting called the Mona Lisa. 'Do you know who painted this?' he asks. My best friend, An, puts his hand up and says, 'Yes! It was painted by Leonardo da Vinci.' Mr. David smiles and says, 'Very good, An!'

Next, we work in pairs to make friends and draw portraits. An has got dark eyes and a kind smile. He draws a green kiwi and a cute jellyfish, while I draw my sister playing the piano. Everyone is happy to tell the class about their drawings.
