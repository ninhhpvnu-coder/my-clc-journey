🏖️ Building Sandcastles on Our Summer Beach Holiday

Every summer, our family takes a wonderful holiday to Da Nang Beach.

The weather is sunny and warm. Before heading out, mum reminds us, 'Wear your sun hats and remember to drink plenty of water!' We run cheerfully across the soft white sand. The turquoise sea is calm, and there aren't any big waves near the shore.

My younger brother and I start building a huge sandcastle with four round towers. We walk closer to the water's edge to collect thirty shiny sea shells and smooth pebbles to decorate our castle walls. When my brother sees a small wooden boat far out at sea, he asks our English-speaking tour guide, 'How do you say 'con thuyền' in English?' The guide replies with a warm smile, 'It's 'boat', spelled B-O-A-T.' Learning English while playing by the sea is the best holiday ever!
