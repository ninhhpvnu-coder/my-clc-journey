A Great Journey Around the World

My name is Lucas, and I love to travel around the world. Last year, my family flew from the US to Europe. We visited Spain and Poland, where the local people were very friendly. It was not difficult to make friends with other children in the parks.

Next month, we will visit Asia and Oceania. My dream is to see Japan and then arrive in Australia. I want to see a cute koala in a tall tree because I have never seen one before! My father says every country has a unique culture. Our journey will end in Indonesia before we fly home. Tomorrow, I will prepare my backpack.
