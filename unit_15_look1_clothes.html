🐸 The Tale of the Two Frogs in the Milk Bucket

Once upon a time, two green frogs accidentally fell into a deep wooden bucket filled with fresh milk.

The sides of the bucket were high and slippery. The first frog thought, 'There is no hope. We cannot jump out!' It stopped kicking its legs and sadly sank to the bottom.

However, the second frog was brave and determined. It thought, 'I must never give up! As long as I have strength, I will keep kicking!' It kicked its strong legs round and round, churning the creamy liquid tirelessly. Slowly but surely, the milk began to thicken and turned into a solid block of delicious butter! The clever frog stepped onto the firm butter, gave a mighty leap, and shouted, 'Hooray!' as it jumped out to freedom. Perseverance always brings victory!
