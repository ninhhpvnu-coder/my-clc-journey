A Day at the Green School

Welcome to Green School in the countryside! This is not a normal school. The classrooms do not have walls or windows. They are completely open, so fresh air comes in all day.

Every student has got a wooden desk and a colourful school bag. From Monday to Friday, children learn many exciting lessons here. They study reading and maths, but they also learn about nature.

Next to the classroom, there is a big green garden. Students water young plants and pick yellow flowers. They also grow rice and sweet fruit. The garden gives healthy food to all the children. Everyone feels happy at this special school.
