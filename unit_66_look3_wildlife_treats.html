A Visit to the Coastal Wildlife Centre

Last Tuesday, our class visited a famous marine wildlife centre in central Vietnam. We were excited to learn about different wild creatures and their natural behaviour.

First, our guide showed us how friendly dolphins jump gracefully out of the water. We also watched cute penguins swim fast to stay safe and warm. Nearby, a large exhibition taught us that the blue whale is the biggest animal on Earth, while sharks hunt when they feel hungry.

At midday, we stopped at a cosy seaside diner. We could choose delicious food and order a cold mint smoothie with sweet yoghurt. It was not boring at all; we had an incredible day discovering ocean life!
