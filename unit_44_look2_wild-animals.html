A Safari Adventure in Green Village

Tom and his family are visiting a safari park near a green village. A kind zoo worker welcomes every guest at the gate. Tom wears a warm jacket with a notebook in his pocket.

First, they see a tall giraffe eating leaves and a heavy rhino drinking water. Next, they watch a big lion under a rock. Tom whispers, "Shh! Is it sleeping?" His sister laughs, "Yes, it is."

Nearby, a green snake glides past a pond where a crocodile snaps its jaws. Two striped zebras run fast across the grass, but a fat hippo is very slow. A funny monkey swings overhead, making everyone smile.
