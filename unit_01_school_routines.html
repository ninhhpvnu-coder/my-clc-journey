Nguyen Tat Thanh School is one of the most reputable schools in Hanoi. Every day, students arrive at school before 7:30 a.m. dressed neatly in their crisp uniforms.

The daily school routine begins with a lively 15-minute morning exercise on the spacious playground. Students study key academic subjects like Mathematics, Vietnamese, and English in the morning.

In the afternoon, they carry out interesting science experiments in modern laboratories and work on group projects in the digital library. Teachers always encourage students to concentrate on creative thinking rather than memorizing facts.

After academic hours, there are diverse extracurricular activities, including basketball, arts, and robotics clubs. Many students work diligently to achieve excellent results at the end of each semester.

Dạng bài phân loại điểm 9-10. Gõ câu hoàn chỉnh vào ô bên dưới và bấm "Kiểm Tra".

🎙️ UMS & CLC Admission Interview Practice

Mô phỏng phỏng vấn vấn đáp tiếng Anh vào trường CLC. Bấm nghe câu hỏi và câu trả lời mẫu:

Question 1: Can you introduce yourself and describe your daily school routine?
💡 Gợi ý trả lời mẫu: "Good morning teachers. My name is Minh. Every morning, I wake up at 6:30 and prepare my uniform. At school, I usually attend academic classes in the morning and participate in our English speaking club in the afternoon..."
Question 2: What is your favorite school subject and why do you love it?
💡 Gợi ý trả lời mẫu: "My favorite subject is English because it allows me to explore fascinating science books from National Geographic and connect with friends from all over the world..."
