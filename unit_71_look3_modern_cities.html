Life in Tokyo: A Modern Megacity

Kenji lives with his parents in a comfortable apartment on the tenth floor of a tall skyscraper in Tokyo. Tokyo is a bustling megacity where millions of people live and work. In the past, people walked long distances, but these days life is much faster. Every morning, Kenji and his classmates walk to school together, while his father walks to the nearby subway station to catch a train to his technology company. In the afternoon, Kenji exercises at the local sports centre or visits the shopping centre with his mother. Many international tourists love Tokyo because they can compare stunning modern buildings with peaceful ancient temples in the city square.
