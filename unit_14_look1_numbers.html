🌾 A Sunny Morning Counting Animals at Green Meadow Farm

Today is Saturday, and my cousin and I are visiting Green Meadow Farm to help farmer John.

First, farmer John gives us a fun counting game. 'How many different animals can you find?' he asks with a kind smile. We walk around the farm and start counting. Inside the warm sheep pen, there are eighteen gentle sheep and two fluffy white lambs. That makes twenty sheep altogether!

Next, we head towards the clear duck pond. Twelve colourful ducks are swimming smoothly, while bees buzz around the yellow sunflowers. 'Can ducks fly?' my cousin asks. 'Of course! Ducks can fly very high when they migrate,' farmer John explains. We love feeding the animals and watching them enjoy their delicious food under the warm sun.
