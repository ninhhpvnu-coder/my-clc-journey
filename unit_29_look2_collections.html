Claudia's Tidy Bedroom

Today, the weather is rainy, so Claudia and her brother stay at home. Mother says, "Everyone, please tidy up your rooms first!" Claudia's bedroom is not tidy. There are lots of toys on the floor, including a soft teddy bear, two yellow rubber ducks, and a toy queen.

Claudia says to her brother, "That is a good idea. Can you help me?" Her brother says, "OK! Let's put them into the big box." Together, they jump up to reach the top shelf and put the books away. Claudia puts her small computer on the desk.

Now the room looks neat and clean. Father comes in and says, "I know you worked hard! Now we can watch a funny video together."
