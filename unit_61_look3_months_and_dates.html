Winter in the Far North

In the freezing north of Europe, winter arrives early. From November to February, thick white snow covers the whole mountain region. For the Sami people, the reindeer is an extremely important animal. These strong creatures help families travel across the ice and provide warm fur for clothes. Every year, on the twentieth of December, children receive a special invitation to the winter festival. At the festival, visitors find out how indigenous people live and celebrate the winter solstice around the twenty-first of the month. Although January brings freezing winds and dark nights, people stay cheerful by singing traditional songs together. When March comes, sunlight slowly returns.
