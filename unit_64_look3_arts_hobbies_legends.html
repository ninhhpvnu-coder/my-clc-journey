A Talented Young Artist in Finland

Last winter, Oliver traveled to Finland with his family to enter a national art competition. Oliver is a young artist who is famous for his lifelike paintings. Looking through the icy hotel window, he saw tall pine trees and a little white goat standing near the fence. Because it was freezing outside, Oliver put on a heavy coat and walked quietly to the old palace in the city centre. Inside the palace museum, he admired a grand portrait of an ancient emperor and his wise advisor. Oliver felt inspired and painted the snowy garden. When the judges saw his artwork, they began to smile and laugh with delight. Oliver won first prize, and his parents were extremely proud of his great achievement.
