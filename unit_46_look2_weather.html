A Rainy Afternoon and a Beautiful Rainbow

Yesterday afternoon, the weather changed very quickly. At two o’clock, the sky turned dark and a heavy storm arrived. It was cold and windy outside. Mum said, "Don’t forget to bring an umbrella and wear a coat when you leave school."

My brother Minh put on a warm scarf by himself because he was cold. Soon, large raindrops fell on the windows and the garden paths. After thirty minutes, the rain stopped and bright sunlight returned. The sky became brighter than before. Suddenly, we saw a giant rainbow with a soft violet arc across the blue sky. It was truly a wonderful sight.
