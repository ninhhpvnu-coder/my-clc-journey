🤿 Snorkelling in the Crystal Waters of the Caribbean Sea

During our summer vacation, my family traveled to a tropical island surrounded by the warm Caribbean Sea.

The weather was hot and clear. In the morning, our instructor gave each of us a mask, a yellow snorkel, and a pair of rubber flippers. 'Are there any sharks here?' I asked nervously. 'No, there aren't,' the instructor smiled. 'You will only see gentle sea turtles and hundreds of tiny neon fish!'

We swam into the shallow turquoise bay and put our faces into the water. It was magical! I looked for sea stars resting on white sand. In our free time in the afternoon, we flew a kite on the windy shore, drank fresh lemonade from a glass jug, and wrote stories about our underwater exploration in our travel journals. It was the most exciting adventure of my life!
