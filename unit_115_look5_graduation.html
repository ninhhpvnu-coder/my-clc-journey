The Guardians of the Blue Glacier

High up in the northern mountains, a team of daring environmental researchers arrived to study a massive glacier. A thick sheet of glistening blue ice stretched across the rocky valley for miles. As night approached, the icy wind began to blow violently across the frozen peaks, causing temperatures to freeze every puddle of water in minutes. The two youngest researchers began to argue heatedly about setting up camp immediately, as they were frightened of the approaching blizzard. However, the veteran expedition leader encouraged everyone to remain calm and continue their descent toward a natural hollow sheltered by stone. There, sheltered from the gale, they watched the crimson sun set over the horizon. Inside a protected limestone cave, they discovered extraordinary symbols that prehistoric hunters had chosen to carve thousands of years earlier. Just as a desert oasis offers life-saving sanctuary to weary caravans, this warm cave allowed the frozen crew to rest safely before continuing their vital climate observations the next morning.
