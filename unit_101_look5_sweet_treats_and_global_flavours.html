The Global Youth Flavours Fair

Every spring, culinary academies across Europe gather for the Global Youth Flavours Fair, where gifted students aged twelve to sixteen demonstrate their cooking skills. Under the guidance of an executive chef, participants prepare traditional dishes from various cultures. Visitors can browse a vibrant printed menu before visiting each food stall. At the Asian culinary section, young cooks toss noodles in sizzling woks and assemble delicate sushi rolls using raw salmon and crisp seaweed. Others shape tender beef meatballs simmered with sweet green peas and button mushrooms. Nearby, visitors crack open crisp fortune cookies to read inspiring proverbs before tasting savoury broths with a wooden spoon or picking up dumplings with a silver fork. For dessert lovers, the festival offers an enticing treat. A Dutch team uses a heated frying pan to bake thin waffles with a rich filling of melted caramel and a tiny pinch of sea salt. Nutritionists at the event emphasize the importance of eating healthily, encouraging diners to choose a balanced portion of fresh grapes and creamy yoghurt rather than consuming excessive refined sugar. Many enthusiastic foodies eagerly look forward to this delicious celebration every year.
