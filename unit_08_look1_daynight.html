☀️ Day and Night in the Secret Garden

Day and night in our garden are very different, but both are full of magical life.

During the day, the golden sun shines over the flowers. My playful dog runs across the green grass. Colorful butterflies flutter around, and little birds sing from the trees. Humans use their hands and legs to work, exercise, and play.

At night, when the sky turns dark and the silver moon appears, humans go to sleep in bed. But the garden is not empty! A cute hedgehog crawls out from under the bushes to look for food. An owl opens its large eyes and listens with its sharp ears. The nighttime nature is quiet and wonderful.
