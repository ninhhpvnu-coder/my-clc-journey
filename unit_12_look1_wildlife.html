🦁 A Day at the Savanna Wildlife Park and Our Town

Last Saturday, my family took a weekend trip to the Savanna Wildlife Park located just outside our town.

First, our open-top safari bus drove along a sandy stone path surrounded by a protective fence. 'Look around, children!' the guide said. In the golden savanna grass, a majestic lion rested under a baobab tree while two fluffy cubs played with their mother's long tail. Nearby, a group of funny meerkats stood upright on their hind legs like tiny soldiers.

In the afternoon, we returned to our peaceful town centre. There's a spacious library next to the park, where we borrowed nature magazines. Then, my brother and I enjoyed swimming at the town swimming pool. We love our town and cherish all the wonderful animals in nature!
