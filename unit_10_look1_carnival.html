🏡 The Magical Carnival Masks in Our House

Tomorrow is the school Carnival, and our house on Nguyen Chi Thanh Street is full of vibrant craft materials.

In the living room, dad and I sit at the large wooden table next to the aquarium. We are making two giant dragon masks out of recycled cardboard and glittering paper. There is a vase of yellow flowers on the table and a loud clock ticking on the wall.

'Where's my tube of gold paint?' I ask. Mum calls from the kitchen, 'Look under your chair!' Sure enough, the paint tube is lying under my stool. Next, we paint shining scales on the dragons and let them dry on the balcony. Everyone who sees the masks smiles with excitement. We can't wait for tomorrow's Carnival parade!
