📖 My New Friend at School

Hello! My name is Minh, and I am ten years old. Today is my first day in the new school year. In my class, there are thirty children: fifteen boys and fifteen girls.

I have a new best friend. His name is Nam. Nam is ten years old, too. He has a very cool school bag. His bag is bright blue, and he has four sharp pencils: one red, one green, one yellow, and one black. Nam likes to draw pictures of animals.

Our teacher is Miss Lan. She is very kind and friendly. She welcomes all the children with a warm smile. I am very happy to study here!
