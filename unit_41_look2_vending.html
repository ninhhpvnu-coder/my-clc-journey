🍱 Lunchtime at Green Hill School

It is lunchtime at Green Hill School. Lan opens her lunch box. There’s a rice ball and there are some carrots inside. There isn’t any juice, so she goes to the vending machine. She puts her money in the machine and buys a drink.

Her friend Ben usually eats a burger. Today there aren’t any burgers, so he has a sausage. Ben and Lan share their food. The other children are happy too. Even the teacher smiles!

After lunch, they grab their bags and play outside. Lan is wearing a pink dress and Ben is wearing blue jeans. They both love lunchtime. It is the best part of the school day.
