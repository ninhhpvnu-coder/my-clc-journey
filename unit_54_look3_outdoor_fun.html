A Sunny Day in London Park

David lives in a modern flat in London, the capital of the UK. On Saturday morning, he walks down the quiet street to the central park. He wears blue jeans and a red helmet on his head. At the park, David meets his best friend, Lucas. Lucas is riding a fast bike, while David practices on his new skateboard. The two boys run to the wooden climbing frame and play a game of catch with a tennis ball. Sixty children are playing happily in the sunshine. Lucas’s mother uses a small camera to take a photo of the boys. They have so much fun together before walking home to have lunch in the dining room.
