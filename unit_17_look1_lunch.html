🥗 A Healthy and Cheerful Lunch at the School Canteen

At twelve o'clock, the melodic school bell rings, and it is time for lunch!

All fifth-grade students line up politely outside the canteen. I pick up a clean metal tray and choose my favourite meal. Today's special menu features a fluffy cheese omelette with fresh red tomatoes, steamed rice, and a bowl of green vegetable soup. It smells so nice and looks yummy!

I sit next to my best friend, Nam. 'Do you like vegetables?' I ask him. 'Yes, I do! Carrots and spinach make us strong and fit,' Nam nods with a broad smile. For dessert, the school chef serves ripe bananas and a cup of warm milk. Eating a balanced lunch with friends brings us joy and energy for the afternoon lessons.
