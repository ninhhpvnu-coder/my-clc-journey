Wild Animals and Their Habitats

Last Sunday, our kind teacher took us to a nature center. The weather was cloudy and cooler than the city. First, we visited the desert house to see a rare sand cat. It is smaller than a wild dog, but it can live without much water. Nearby, a green iguana rested quietly on a dry tree branch. Its tail was longer than a lizard's tail.

Next, we walked to a big lake where workers look after funny snow monkeys. A baby monkey ate fruit hungrily beside its mother. My younger brother said the baby monkey was cuter than the iguana. Before we could go off to play snakes and ladders, we decided to stay in the cool shade to take notes.
