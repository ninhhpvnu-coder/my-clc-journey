An Exciting Saturday in Our Neighbourhood

Last Saturday was a memorable day for Peter and his sister. In the morning, they walked downstairs to have breakfast before walking down the street to the neighbourhood playground. Peter brought a shuttlecock to play badminton, while his sister enjoyed skipping rope to keep fit. After that, they stepped into a busy café next to the fruit market to enjoy cold lemonade. At noon, an unusual event happened: a partial solar eclipse! The children put on protective glasses and used a homemade pinhole viewer to watch the bright sun safely. Finally, in the evening, their father drove them to the cinema near the bus stop to watch an adventure movie.
